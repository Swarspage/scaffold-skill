# Typography for immersive pages

Assign roles rather than selecting a fashionable font by default: a display face establishes personality, a body face sustains reading, and an optional mono face carries technical labels. Display may be condensed, extended, editorial, geometric, grotesk, humanist, or high-contrast serif when that choice serves the thesis.

Use fluid sizes with bounded `clamp()` values. Check actual variable-font axes and optical sizing; do not assume a declared weight exists. Keep hero text short, use tracking sparingly, and ensure text on moving canvas regions has an opaque/quiet backing or enough clear space. Do not default to Inter, Poppins, or a single generic sans merely for convenience.
