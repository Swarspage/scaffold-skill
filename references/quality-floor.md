# Quality floor

- No default aesthetic, invented product detail, fake asset availability, or unsupported claim.
- No recognizable complex product represented as arbitrary primitives when fidelity is required.
- No unlicensed external 3D asset or unverified attribution.
- No decorative motion without narrative purpose, desktop-only composition, or hidden mobile canvas as a shortcut.
- No automatic purple/blue gradient, universal glow, or glass-card wallpaper.
- No visual-pass claim based only on build logs, console output, or a WebGL context.
- Respect the supplied brief over personal taste; do not trade usability, accessibility, or performance for spectacle.
