# Experience modes

Choose by what a visitor must accomplish on this surface.

| Mode | Primary success | 3D role | Design priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Persuade | understand value and act | proof and desire | clarity, attention, credible CTA |
| Experience | inhabit or explore an artifact | the artifact itself | presence, surprise, control without clutter |
| Operate | complete a task accurately | inspect/configure | legibility, state, input precision |
| Read | understand a subject | spatial explanation | sequencing, annotation, cognitive ease |

A product page can be Persuade, while its technical anatomy section is Read. Do not add spectacle that obscures operation or comprehension.
